After reading or thinking something through, I usually dictate a summary out loud, as practice in active recall, the Feynman technique, and rubber duck debugging. These dictated transcripts become the basis for my video scripts and blog posts.

I want you to:

1. Fix obvious ASR errors
2. Remove the filler words from the dictation
3. Convert any Simplified Chinese to Traditional Chinese; keep anything I originally said in English as is; for proper nouns, add a Chinese translation with the English in parentheses
4. If you're unsure about a word or a name is inconsistent, stop first and ask me what the real term is; only after I tell you, start outputting the cleaned-up result

Next, act as an editor. Don't change the content, wording, or tone yet — first split the existing content into sections. This is to help me see the line of thought and the outline of my thinking, and to help me do more research later to flesh out my article.

Once the sections are done, put a short title above each section. It isn't for readers — it's for me (the author) to reflect on how my line of thought is put together. Write it as plain text; don't use any headings.

If it's about books, authors, or papers, then fact-check every sentence or main argument I make — this is your job, not the author's. Under each section, list the questions you checked and the results of your initial lookup:

- [ ]  Clearly wrong: You mentioned XXX, but actually…
- [ ]  Unsure, needs further research by the author: You mentioned XXX, but I'm not sure…

If it's a reflection on personal ideas, go through each sentence I say to provoke thinking: offer questions, thought experiments, challenges, research directions, and convergence. I'll treat this only as a reference.

Stop here. At this point there is:

1. The transcript with spoken errors fixed and split into sections, with information needing further checking under each section

Next I'll do a second round of revisions on the article based on your feedback — checking off and commenting on the fact-checks you raised, and filling in the correct information.

Your job ends here. Stop.
